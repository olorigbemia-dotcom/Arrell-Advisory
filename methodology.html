Our approach

Business outcomes set the direction.

AI is one possible intervention. We start by understanding your organization’s priorities and the work that produces its current results.

The adoption journey

Enter where you need support.

  1. 01Learn
  2. 02Assess
  3. 03Align
  4. 04Design
  5. 05Adopt
  6. 06Enable
  7. 07Govern
  8. 08Improve

Start where your organization needs support. Learning is one entry point; it is not a prerequisite.

01

Learn

Build enough understanding to participate in AI decisions. Skip this stage when the team is already prepared.

02

Assess

Understand leadership, strategy, workflow, people, governance, and data readiness.

03

Align

Define organizational outcomes and the problems worth solving.

04

Design

Map the workflow. Compare AI and non-AI options, then decide what to automate and what to augment.

05

Adopt

Plan a bounded pilot with clear responsibilities. Technical implementation stays with your technical team or providers.

06

Enable

Prepare people for their roles, develop practical skills, and reinforce responsible habits.

07

Govern

Embed ownership, review, data boundaries, and escalation into the work. These considerations begin in discovery and develop through the journey.

08

Improve

Measure against the intended outcome. Learn, adapt, expand, or stop based on evidence.

Governance throughout

Responsibility develops with the work.

Governance is considered from the first decision, then made concrete in the pilot, roles, data use, and operating practices. It is not a final approval stage added after adoption.
