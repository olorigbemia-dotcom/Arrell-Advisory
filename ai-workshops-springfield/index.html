Frequently asked questions

Do I need AI or coding experience?

No. The workshops are designed for non-technical business owners and teams. Bring your laptop and follow the guided implementation.

Do I have to attend all three?

No. Each lab solves a distinct business problem. Choose the workshop most relevant to your business or attend all three.

Who are the labs for?

Small business owners, entrepreneurs, managers and teams in Springfield, Republic, Nixa, Ozark and the surrounding Southwest Missouri area.

What should I bring?

A laptop, access to your AI account and relevant non-sensitive business materials. Avoid sharing confidential customer or employee data.
