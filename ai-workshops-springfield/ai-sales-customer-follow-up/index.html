Make every inquiry count.

Potential customers can slip through the cracks when inquiries pile up and follow-ups are inconsistent. Build a practical system that helps you respond, recommend next steps and track opportunities.

What you will learn

  • Map the path from first inquiry to sale.
  • Create an approved service and FAQ knowledge base.
  • Draft tailored replies and follow-up sequences.
  • Set up a lightweight lead tracker and reminder process.
  • Test inquiries, quotes and escalation rules before using the workflow.

What you will build

  • An AI-assisted inquiry and response workflow
  • A customized FAQ and follow-up sequence
  • A lightweight lead tracker
  • A reusable quote or proposal draft process

Who should attend

Small business owners, entrepreneurs and teams across Springfield, Republic, Nixa, Ozark and Southwest Missouri. No coding or prior AI experience required.

Prepare for your lab

Bring a laptop, a list of services and anonymized or fictional examples of customer inquiries. Use only non-sensitive business information. Bring access to an AI account.

Small business owners, entrepreneurs, managers and teams in Springfield, Republic, Nixa, Ozark and the surrounding Southwest Missouri area.
