Reclaim time in your business.

When you run a small business, email, planning, staff communication and routine administration can take time away from serving customers. Build a practical assistant that helps you handle recurring tasks with less manual effort.

What you will learn

  • Identify repetitive tasks that AI can help complete.
  • Configure an assistant around your business, policies and priorities.
  • Build workflows for email drafts, planning and operational documentation.
  • Set up a recurring business briefing or reminder where supported.
  • Test the outputs and keep decisions under your control.

What you will build

  • A personalized AI business assistant
  • Three reusable administrative workflows
  • A daily or weekly business briefing
  • A repeatable task process and approval checklist

Who should attend

Small business owners, entrepreneurs and teams across Springfield, Republic, Nixa, Ozark and Southwest Missouri. No coding or prior AI experience required.

Prepare for your lab

Bring a laptop, a short description of your business, and examples of routine tasks you would like to delegate. Use only non-sensitive business information. Bring access to an AI account.

No. The workshops are designed for non-technical business owners and teams. Bring your laptop and follow the guided implementation.

No. Each lab solves a distinct business problem. Choose the workshop most relevant to your business or attend all three.

Who are the labs for?

Small business owners, entrepreneurs, managers and teams in Springfield, Republic, Nixa, Ozark and the surrounding Southwest Missouri area.

What should I bring?

A laptop, access to your AI account and relevant non-sensitive business materials. Avoid sharing confidential customer or employee data.
