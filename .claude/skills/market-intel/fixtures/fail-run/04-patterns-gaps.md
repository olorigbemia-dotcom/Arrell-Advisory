# Patterns and gaps

## Facts

Two of two firms in this set describe themselves with a governance or enablement noun and both promise board-level defensibility. [[example-advisory]] [[second-firm]]
Pricing is published on a citable page by 1 of 2 firms, and only one specific figure is recoverable across the whole set. https://secondfirm.com/services
Both firms address the Chief Risk Officer as the named buyer, and neither states a target organization size anywhere in public copy. https://example.com/services

## Interpretations

The market is converging on assessment-led delivery and most firms typically charges a fixed fee for the first engagement.
The leading firms in this category appear to treat pricing opacity as a positioning choice rather than an oversight, which suggests the buyer is expected to self-qualify before contact.

## Guesses

Pricing opacity may indicate that engagement scope varies enough that a published figure would misrepresent the work. This is unverified.
